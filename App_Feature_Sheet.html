App Feature Sheet

Everything the app does today

A full rundown of what's built and live at fresas-app-zeta.vercel.app — from the customer ordering experience to the tools running behind the scenes.

🛒Ordering — Customer Side

  • Fully bilingual site — customers switch between English and Spanish anytime
  • 6 flavors to choose from: Regular, Raffaello, Biscoff, Ferrero Rocher, Banana Pudding & Gansito
  • 12 oz or 24 oz cup size, with pricing that updates instantly
  • Pick toppings & syrups, with free toppings included depending on flavor
  • Optional flavored cup rim for Banana Pudding & Gansitocustomer chooses rim on/off, e.g. lechera & crushed Nilla wafer
  • Automatic allergy alert for walnuts in the Ferrero Rocher flavor
  • Repeat-customer shortcutauto-fills a returning customer's usual order by phone number
  • "Add to Home Screen" tutorialwalks customers through installing the site like an app, right from their phone's browser

📅Scheduling & Payment

  • Pick a pickup date — today or up to 30 days ahead
  • Pickup time slots generated from your own store hoursweekday hours can differ from weekend hours
  • Time slots automatically hide once they're full or already passed
  • Pay by Zelle or cash, with a confirmation step before the order sends
  • Promo code field right in the order review stepinstant discount, validated live against your active codes
  • Clean order confirmation screen with pickup date, time & address

🔔Automatic Notifications

  • Push notification to your phone the instant a new order comes in
  • Bilingual text to the customer when they order
  • A second text when their order is marked ready for pickup

🔐Admin Dashboard

  • Password-protected login, separate from the customer site
  • Live order feed that refreshes automatically
  • Mark orders ready/done and paid/unpaid with one tap
  • Edit any order's details after it's placed
  • Delete one or multiple orders at once
  • Open or close the shop with a custom message & "back at" time

🧾Menu & Availability Controls

  • Mark any flavor, topping, or syrup sold out in real time — customers instantly can't select it
  • Set your catering availability days & a custom info message
  • Create & manage promo codespercent or dollar off, optional expiration date, usage cap, and an on/off switch
  • Collapsible sections keep the dashboard tidy on a phone screen

📊Sales & Reporting

  • Sales dashboard — today, last 7 days, and last 90 days at a glance
  • Best-selling flavors and busiest pickup times
  • Full order history exported to Excel, with date-range filters

🌐Site Pages

  • Home — today's hours, open/closed status, quick links to order or see catering info
  • Order — the full order builder described above
  • Catering — availability & info, shown automatically once you've set it
  • Photos — a gallery of shop photos, uploaded straight from the admin dashboard
