📱 Custom Order AppBuilt for small businesses that take orders
Feature Overview

Everything this app can do for your business

A ready-to-customize ordering system — built once, adapted to fit your menu, your hours, and your workflow. Here's what's included.

🛒Ordering — Customer Side

  • Fully bilingual site — customers switch languages anytime
  • Custom menu built around your productsflavors, sizes, add-ons — whatever you sell
  • Multiple size/option tiers, with pricing that updates instantly
  • Pick toppings, extras, or add-ons, with items included free by default when you want
  • Optional prompts for special optionse.g. a customer choosing an extra or upgrade before checkout
  • Automatic alerts for allergens or special ingredients
  • Repeat-customer shortcutauto-fills a returning customer's usual order by phone number
  • "Add to Home Screen" tutorialwalks customers through installing the site like an app, right from their phone's browser

📅Scheduling & Payment

  • Pick a pickup or service date — today or scheduled ahead
  • Time slots generated from your own business hoursweekday hours can differ from weekend hours
  • Time slots automatically hide once they're full or already passed
  • Pay by Zelle, cash, or card, with a confirmation step before the order sends
  • Promo code field right in the order review stepinstant discount, validated live against your active codes
  • Clean order confirmation screen with date, time & details

🔔Automatic Notifications

  • Push notification to your phone the instant a new order comes in
  • Bilingual text to the customer when they order
  • A second text when their order is marked ready

🔐Admin Dashboard

  • Password-protected login, separate from the customer site
  • Live order feed that refreshes automatically
  • Mark orders ready/done and paid/unpaid with one tap
  • Edit any order's details after it's placed
  • Delete one or multiple orders at once
  • Open or close for business with a custom message & "back at" time

🧾Menu & Availability Controls

  • Mark any item sold out in real time — customers instantly can't select it
  • Set special availability windows & a custom info message
  • Create & manage promo codespercent or dollar off, optional expiration date, usage cap, and an on/off switch
  • Collapsible sections keep the dashboard tidy on a phone screen

📊Sales & Reporting

  • Sales dashboard — today, last 7 days, and last 90 days at a glance
  • Best-sellers and busiest times, tracked automatically
  • Full order history exported to Excel, with date-range filters

🌐Site Pages

  • Home — hours, open/closed status, quick links to order or learn more
  • Order — the full order builder described above
  • Info page — availability & details, shown automatically when set
  • Gallery — a photo gallery, updated right from the dashboard
